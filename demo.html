No. This is an illustrative product catalogue and basket demo, not a live marketplace.

No. Demo login does not verify or store passwords. Use a made-up password only.

Can the AI scanner diagnose rice disease?

Not currently. A validated trained model has not been integrated, and the upload only previews your photo.
